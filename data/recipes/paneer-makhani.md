## Ingredients

### For the paneer tikka:

- Paneer (500g, cut into large cubes)
- Hung yoghurt (200g)
- Mustard oil (30g)
- Kashmiri chilli powder (30g)
- Ginger garlic paste (30g)
- Coriander powder (15g)
- Kosher salt (10g, adjust to taste)
- Garam masala (5g)
- Cumin powder (5g)
- Turmeric powder (3g)
- Kasoori methi (3g, dried fenugreek leaves)
- Amchur powder (3g, dried mango powder)
- Black salt (3g)
- Lemon juice (15ml, ideally Indian lemon; substitute: 45% lime + 35% lemon + 20% white grapefruit juice)

### For the makhani sauce:

- Fresh San Marzano tomatoes (1000g, chopped)
- Shallots (400g, sliced)
- Double cream (100g)
- Salted butter (80g)
- Roasted cashew nuts (50g)
- Coriander stems (50g, chopped)
- Ghee (30g)
- Kashmiri chilli powder (30g)
- Garlic (20g, chopped)
- Ginger (20g, chopped)
- Coriander powder (15g)
- Green bird's eye chilli (5g, chopped)
- Cumin seeds (5g)
- Whole Kashmiri chillies (5)
- Green cardamom (12 pods)
- Kasoori methi (3g)
- Khoya (3g, milk solids)
- Turmeric powder (3g)
- Salt (to taste)

## Special equipment

- High-speed blender (a Vitamix works well)
- Skewers

## Instructions

### Paneer tikka:

- Gently mix the paneer cubes with the ginger garlic paste, kosher salt, and lemon juice. Let rest for 15-20 minutes.

- Make the marinade: in a large bowl, combine the hung yoghurt, mustard oil, Kashmiri chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, turmeric, kasoori methi, amchur powder, black salt, and garam masala into a thick paste.

- Add the paneer to the marinade and fold gently to coat without breaking the cubes. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or ideally overnight.

- Skewer the marinated paneer and grill under the oven broiler at maximum heat until lightly charred around the edges. Set aside.

### Makhani sauce:

- Heat ghee in a large pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle.

- Add garlic, ginger, green chilli, and shallots. Cook on medium heat until the onions are translucent — do not brown them.

- Add the Kashmiri chilli powder, coriander powder, turmeric, whole Kashmiri chillies, cardamom, tomatoes, cashews, coriander stems, and khoya. Cook on low heat for 30-40 minutes until the tomatoes are completely soft.

- Add the butter, cream, and kasoori methi. Blend the entire mixture in a high-speed blender until velvety smooth.

### Assembly:

- Return the blended sauce to the pot. Add the grilled paneer cubes.

- Bring to a gentle simmer to reheat the paneer and allow the flavors to meld.

- Adjust seasoning with salt and more kasoori methi if needed. Serve hot with naan or rice.
