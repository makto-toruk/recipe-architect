## Ingredients

- Baby broccoli / broccolini (1 1/2 lbs, trimmed)
- Olive oil (3 Tbsp)
- Garlic powder (1/2 tsp)
- Kosher salt (1/2 tsp)
- Black pepper (1/2 tsp)
- Walnut pieces (1/3 cup)

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.

- Toss the trimmed broccolini with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated. Spread in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.

- Roast for 12–15 minutes until the stems are tender and the florets are starting to crisp at the edges.

- Scatter walnut pieces over the pan and roast for another 5 minutes until the walnuts are toasted and the broccoli edges are golden. Serve immediately.
