## Ingredients

- Coconut (3/4 cup)
- Peanuts (2 Tbsp)
- Roasted channa dal (2 Tbsp)
- Green chillies (2)
- Garlic cloves (5)
- Salt (to taste)
- Black peppercorns (6)
- Cumin seeds (1/2 tsp)
- Jaggery (a pinch)
- Tamarind (1 Tbsp)
- Cilantro (1/2 cup)
- Mint leaves (1/2 cup)
- Curry leaves (8)
- Water (about 1/2 cup)

## Special equipment

- Blender

## Instructions

- Dry-roast the peanuts and roasted channa dal.

- Add the roasted peanuts and roasted channa dal to a blender with the coconut, green chillies, garlic, salt, peppercorns, cumin seeds, jaggery, tamarind, cilantro, mint, and curry leaves.

- Add the water gradually and blend until combined but still slightly coarse; do not blend until smooth.
