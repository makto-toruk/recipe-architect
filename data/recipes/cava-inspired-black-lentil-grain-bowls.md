## Ingredients

### Roasted vegetables:

- Olive oil (2 Tbsp, divided)
- Carrots (1 lb, chopped to 1")
- Brussels sprouts (1 lb, halved)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp, divided)
- Cumin (1 tsp, divided)
- Paprika (1 tsp, divided)
- Ground black pepper (1/2 tsp, divided)
- Garlic powder (1/2 tsp, divided)

### Black lentils:

- Black lentils (1 cup)
- Olive oil (1 Tbsp)
- Sweet onion (1/2 medium, finely chopped)
- Garlic cloves (2, minced)
- Water or broth (3 cups)
- Bay leaf (1)
- Kosher salt (1/2 tsp)

### Herb quinoa:

- Quinoa (1 cup)
- Water or broth (1 1/2 cups)
- Salt (1/2 tsp)
- Fresh parsley (2 Tbsp, minced)
- Fresh mint (2 Tbsp, minced)

### Toppings:

- Hummus (1/2 cup)
- Tzatziki or Greek yogurt (1/2 cup)
- Pickled red onions (1/2 cup)
- Kalamata olives (8 oz)

## Instructions

- Heat oven to 425°F. Toss carrots with 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, and 1/4 tsp garlic powder. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 15 minutes.

- Add Brussels sprouts to the pan, toss with remaining oil and spices, and roast 5–7 minutes more until vegetables are tender and caramelized.

- While vegetables roast, rinse the lentils and discard any broken pieces. In a saucepan, sauté the onion in olive oil until soft, then add garlic. Add lentils, water or broth, and bay leaf. Simmer 25 minutes until bite-tender. Remove bay leaf, drain excess liquid, and stir in salt.

- Rinse quinoa thoroughly. Combine with water or broth and salt; bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and cook 15 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Rest 5 minutes covered, then fluff and stir in parsley and mint.

- Assemble bowls: layer 3/4 cup quinoa and 2/3 cup lentils in each bowl. Add roasted vegetables. Top with hummus, tzatziki, pickled onions, and olives.
