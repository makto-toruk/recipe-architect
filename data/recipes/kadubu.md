## Ingredients

### For the filling:

- Peanuts (1/2 cup)
- White sesame seeds (1/2 cup)
- Cardamom pods (5)
- Almonds (10)
- Jaggery (1 cup)

### For the wrapper:

- Water (3 cups)
- Rice flour (1 1/2 cups)
- Salt (a little)

## Special equipment

- Food processor
- Wooden spoon or stirring stick

## Instructions

- Roast the peanuts, sesame seeds, and almonds separately.

- Crush the cardamom, then grind it with the almonds in a food processor. Add the peanuts and sesame seeds and pulse again, keeping the mixture slightly coarse.

- Add the jaggery and pulse briefly to combine. Shape the filling into about 10 balls.

- Bring the water to a boil with a little salt. Add the rice flour and stir with a wooden spoon or stirring stick until it comes together as a wet dough ball.

- Keep the dough covered. Working with one portion at a time, flatten the dough into a round wrapper, place a ball of filling in the center, and fold the wrapper over it to form a half-moon. Seal and crimp the curved edge. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling.
