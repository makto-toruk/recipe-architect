## Ingredients

- Oil (1 tsp, divided)
- Ladies Finger (1 lb, cleaned, wiped dry, and chopped)
- Mustard Seeds (1/2 tsp)
- Cumin Seeds (1/2 tsp)
- Hing (a pinch)
- Onion (1, diced)
- Curry Leaves (1 sprig)
- Tomato (1-2, chopped)
- Salt (to taste)
- Turmeric (1/4 tsp)
- Coriander Powder (1 tsp)
- Jeera Powder (1/2 tsp)
- Sambar Powder (1 tsp)
- Red Chilli Powder (1/2 tsp)
- Yogurt (1 Tbsp)
- Cilantro (for garnish, optional)

## Instructions

- Wash the ladies finger, wipe completely dry, and chop into pieces.

  - This is important — any moisture will make them slimy.

- Heat 1/2 tsp oil in a pan. Fry the ladies finger until lightly browned, then move them aside to a plate.

- In the same pan, heat the remaining 1/2 tsp oil. Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and hing. Let them splutter.

- Add onion and curry leaves. Sauté until the onion is translucent.

- Add tomato, salt, turmeric, coriander powder, jeera powder, sambar powder, and red chilli powder. Cook until the tomato softens.

- Add the fried ladies finger back to the pan and mix well. Adjust salt to taste.

- Lower the heat. Add yogurt and stir through — it will dry away into the dish.

- Cover with a lid and cook on low heat for about 5 minutes.

- Garnish with cilantro if desired.
