## Ingredients

- Brown rice (1 cup)
- Channa dal (1/2 cup)
- Urad dal (1/2 cup)
- Green gram (1/2 cup)
- Flattened rice (1/2 cup)
- Fenugreek seeds (1 tsp)
- Salt (1 tsp)
- Yeast (1-2g)
- Water (as needed)

## Special equipment

- Blender

## Instructions

- Place the brown rice in a bowl and add enough water for the water level to sit 1-2 inches above the rice. In a separate bowl, add the channa dal, urad dal, green gram, flattened rice, and fenugreek seeds, then add enough water for the water level to sit 1-2 inches above the ingredients. Let both soak for 3-4 hours.

- Grind the soaked ingredients with the salt, using all of the soaking water. Add more water only if needed to form a batter.

- Combine the batter and give it a quick mix with your hands.

  - Mixing the batter by hand is supposed to help with fermentation.

- Add the yeast and mix well. Cover and let the batter ferment overnight.
