## Ingredients

### For the greens:

- Spinach (8 oz, cleaned and roughly chopped)
- Water (1/4 cup)
- Salt (1 tsp, or to taste)
- Black Pepper Powder (1/2 tsp)
- Turmeric (1/8 tsp)

### For the tempering:

- Ghee (2 tsp)
- Mustard Seeds (1/2 tsp)
- Urad Dal (1/2 tsp)
- Cumin Seeds (1/2 tsp)
- Dried Red Chilies (2)

## Instructions

- In a clay pot, add water and chopped spinach along with salt, turmeric, and pepper. Cover and cook over medium heat until the spinach wilts and becomes frothy.

- Reduce heat and mash the spinach using a wooden masher or buttermilk churner until smooth and creamy.

- In a separate small pan, heat ghee. Add mustard seeds, urad dal, cumin seeds, and dried red chilies. Once the mustard seeds splutter and the dal turns golden, pour the tempering over the mashed spinach.

- Mix well and serve hot with steamed rice and ghee.

  - Pairs beautifully with vathal kuzhambu.
