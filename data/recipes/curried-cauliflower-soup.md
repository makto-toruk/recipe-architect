## Ingredients

- Cauliflower (2 medium heads, ~8 cups florets)
- Olive oil (6 Tbsp, divided)
- Onion (2 medium, diced)
- Sweet apple (2 medium, such as Gala, diced)
- Garlic cloves (6, minced)
- Curry powder (3 Tbsp)
- Cumin (2 tsp)
- Turmeric (2 tsp)
- Vegetable or chicken broth (4 cups)
- Reduced-fat coconut milk (1 can, 14 oz)
- Salt (2 tsp)
- Black pepper (2 tsp)
- Rice vinegar (4 tsp)
- Cilantro (for garnish)

## Special equipment

- Immersion blender

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss cauliflower florets with 4 Tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt. Spread on baking sheets and roast for 25–30 minutes until golden brown. Reserve 1–2 cups of roasted florets for garnish.

- Heat remaining 2 Tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onions and apples and sauté until translucent, about 6–7 minutes.

- Add garlic, curry powder, cumin, and turmeric. Cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute.

- Add the roasted cauliflower (minus the reserved amount), broth, coconut milk, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.

- Stir in rice vinegar. Use an immersion blender to blend until smooth.

- Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve topped with reserved roasted cauliflower and cilantro.
