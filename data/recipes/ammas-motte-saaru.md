## Ingredients

### For the masala:

- Onion (1 large, divided)
- Garlic cloves (6, peeled)
- Ginger (2 inches)
- Green chillies (2-3)
- Black pepper (1/2 tsp)
- Curry leaves (a little)
- Cumin seeds (1 tsp)
- Fennel seeds (3/4 tsp)
- Cinnamon (4 pieces)
- Cloves (5)
- Cardamom pods (3)
- Black pepper (2 tsp)
- Cilantro
- Mint leaves
- Almonds (5)
- Cashews (5)
- Coriander powder (2 tsp)

### For the curry:

- Oil (1 tsp)
- Tomato (1)
- Mushrooms (1/2 kg)
- Salt (to taste)
- Water (as needed)
- Eggs (8)

## Special equipment

- Blender
- Wide, deep pan or pressure cooker

## Instructions

- Lightly fry 1/2 of the onion with the garlic, ginger, green chillies, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and curry leaves. Let the mixture cool.

- Add the cooled mixture to a blender with the cumin seeds, fennel seeds, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, 2 tsp black pepper, washed cilantro and mint, almonds, cashews, and coriander powder. Grind to a smooth paste.

- Heat the oil in a wide, deep pan or pressure cooker. Add 1/4 of the onion and lightly sauté it.

- Cut and add the tomato, then add salt to taste. Cut and add the mushrooms.

- Stir everything for 5 minutes, then add the ground masala.

- Add as much water as needed and bring the curry to a boil.

- Lower the heat and crack the eggs into the curry one at a time so that they poach.

- Cook over low heat for 20 minutes, until the eggs are cooked.
